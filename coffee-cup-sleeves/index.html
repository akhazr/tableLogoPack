Custom printed coffee cup sleeves

A printed sleeve is one of the most affordable ways to put your brand on every hot drink. Use them with plain stock cups and switch designs for seasons or promotions.

Options

  • Sleeves sized for standard hot cup sizes
  • Kraft or white sleeves
  • One-colour logos or full-colour designs
  • Space for a website, social handle or QR code
  • Seasonal or limited-edition designs
  • Shipped flat for easy storage behind the counter

How ordering works

Sleeves are a good fit if you're not ready for fully printed cups, or want to change your design more often. Send us your logo and cup sizes and we'll quote sleeves that fit, with a digital proof before printing.

Nothing is printed until you approve your proof.

FAQ

Common questions

Will your sleeves fit my cups?

Tell us the brand or sizes of the cups you use and we'll make sure the sleeves fit.

Can I add a QR code?

Yes. Many cafés add a QR code for their menu, loyalty program or social media.

Are sleeves cheaper than printed cups?

Usually, yes, especially at lower volumes, because you can use plain stock cups and only brand the sleeve.
