FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between a sleeve bag and a takeout bag?

Sleeve bags are flat, open-ended or folded bags for single items like a pastry or sandwich. Takeout bags have a square bottom and handles for full orders.

Do you have grease-resistant bags?

Yes, ask for grease-resistant paper in your quote request, which is ideal for croissants and other buttery items.

Can I get window bags?

Yes. Tell us the size and we'll quote window bags with your logo.
