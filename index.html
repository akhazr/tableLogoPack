FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Who is LogoPack for?

Coffee shops, cafés, restaurants, bakeries, bubble tea shops and food trucks that want their own branded cups, sleeves and paper bags.

What's the minimum order?

It depends on the product and print method. Tell us what you need and roughly how many, and your quote will include options for your volume.

How long does it take?

It depends on the product and quantity. Your quote includes an estimated production and delivery time.

Do I need a designer?

No. Send us your logo, ideally as a PDF, AI, SVG or high-resolution PNG, and we'll place it on your packaging and send a digital proof for approval.

Do you have eco-friendly options?

Tell us if you need recyclable or compostable materials and we'll quote suitable options.

Where are you based?

LogoPack is based in Vancouver, BC. Ask us about delivery to your area.
