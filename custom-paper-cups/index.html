How ordering works

Tell us the sizes you serve and roughly how many cups you go through each month, and send your logo. We'll recommend a cup and print style, quote it, and send a digital proof of your cup so you can see exactly how it will look before anything is printed.

Double-wall cups keep hands comfortable without a sleeve. Single-wall cups paired with a branded sleeve are often the more economical choice.

Nothing is printed until you approve your proof.

FAQ

Common questions

What's the minimum order for custom printed cups?

It depends on the cup type, size and print method. Tell us your sizes and estimated quantities and we'll quote the best option for your volume.

Can you match my brand colours?

Yes. Send your logo and brand colours and we'll match them as closely as the print process allows, and show you a proof first.

Do you have compostable cups?

Tell us if you need recyclable or compostable cups and we'll quote suitable options.
