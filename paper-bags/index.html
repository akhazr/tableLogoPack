FAQ

Common questions

What size bag do I need?

It depends on your containers. Tell us what you pack, for example two drinks or a family meal, and we'll recommend sizes.

Kraft or white bags?

Kraft has a natural, rustic look and hides marks well. White makes colourful logos stand out. We can show you both.

Can you print on both sides?

Yes, ask for one-side or two-side printing in your quote request.
